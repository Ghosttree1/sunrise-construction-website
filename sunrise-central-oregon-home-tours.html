Central Oregon Home Events

Central Oregon Home Tours, Shows & Building Events.

The events worth marking on your calendar if you love homes or you're thinking about building one — the COBA Tour of Homes, the Spring Home & Garden Show, and more — with a builder's take on how to get the most from each.

Central Oregon celebrates homes and building all year — and it's some of the best homework you can do before you build.

Whether you're planning a custom home, remodeling, or simply love good design, the region's home events are a genuine resource. Most are organized by the Central Oregon Builders Association (COBA), a nonprofit trade association chartered in 1972 that runs public showcases alongside its industry work. Walking real homes and talking with real builders teaches you more in an afternoon than months of scrolling.

This is a builder's guide to those events — what they are, roughly when they happen, and how to look at a home the way someone who builds them does. Dates shift year to year, so we link to the official sources for current schedules.

Event details are organized by the Central Oregon Builders Association. Always confirm current dates on the official pages linked below.

COBA Tour of Homes

Central Oregon's flagship home showcase — a free, self-guided tour of newly built homes by the region's top builders, typically spread across two July weekends. It's the best way to walk finished custom homes, compare craftsmanship, and gather ideas in person.

A builder's eye

How to Tour a Home Like a Builder.

Anyone can admire a beautiful kitchen. The more useful visit is the one where you look past the staging at the things that decide how a home actually lives and lasts. A few of the things we notice.

Flow & light

How rooms connect, where daylight lands through the day, and how the home frames its views — the choices you feel every day but rarely see in a photo.

Craft & detailing

Trim, transitions, tile layout, cabinetry reveals, and how materials meet. Clean, considered detailing is the clearest sign of a careful builder.

Site & orientation

How the home sits on its lot — sun, wind, slope, privacy, and outdoor living. A home right for its site feels effortless; one fighting its site never quite does.

The unseen systems

Windows, insulation, mechanical planning, and how a home is built for Central Oregon's snow, sun, and temperature swings. Comfort and durability live here.

Storage & real life

Mudrooms, gear storage, pantries, and the practical spaces a high-desert life needs. Great homes plan for the way people actually live in them.

What it would cost to build

Note the finishes and scope behind the look you love — it's the honest way to connect a tour home to a realistic budget for your own.

Good questions

Central Oregon Home Event FAQs.

When is the COBA Tour of Homes?

The Central Oregon Builders Association (COBA) Tour of Homes is held each July, typically across two weekends, and it's free and open to the public. Dates change year to year, so check coba.org or cobatourofhomes.com for the current schedule.

Is the Tour of Homes free?

Yes. The COBA Tour of Homes is free and open to the public. Only COBA-member builders may showcase a home on the Tour, but anyone can visit and tour the homes during event hours.

When and where is the COBA Home & Garden Show?

COBA hosts a Spring Home & Garden Show each spring, typically at the Deschutes County Fairgrounds & Expo Center in Redmond. It gathers around 200 vendors — builders, remodelers, designers, and home-product suppliers — and draws thousands of visitors. Check coba.org for current dates and details.

What other public events does COBA host?

COBA, a nonprofit trade association chartered in 1972, runs education, networking, and industry events in addition to its public Tour of Homes and Spring Home & Garden Show. The current calendar is published at coba.org/events.

Can I tour a Sunrise-built home?

Sunrise isn't on every year's Tour of Homes, but the homes we have entered have earned Best of Show recognition. When we have a home on the Tour, we'll note it here. Any time of year, you can explore our completed homes through our Selected Work.

How do I get the most out of touring homes?

Look past the staging and styling at the things that determine how a home lives and lasts — craftsmanship and detailing, how spaces connect and capture light and views, the quality of windows, millwork, and finishes, and how well the home suits its site. Those are the differences a builder notices, and they matter far more than trends.
